How an engagement actually starts

No long procurement cycle. We scope the gap on a call, then move.

Scope the gap

We talk through the work that isn't getting done — a stalled migration, a security review, a backlog with no owner — and the skills it actually needs.

Match & onboard

We propose engineers who've done this kind of work before. You interview whoever you like; the ones you choose are working inside your tools within days.

Manage & scale

We handle the employment side and performance check-ins. You keep direction of the work, and you can grow or wind down the team as scope changes.

Staffing, minus the guesswork

Most staffing firms optimize for placement speed. We optimize for whether the person is still useful to you in month four.

Technical vetting, not keyword matching

Every engineer is screened by someone from the same discipline before you ever see a profile — no resume-keyword shortlists.

Single practice leads across all four areas

One point of contact who understands engineering, AI, security and cloud, so you're not re-explaining context to a new account manager each time.

Flexible engagement shapes

One engineer for three months or a five-person embedded pod for two years — the model bends to the problem, not the other way round.

You keep the roadmap

Our engineers work inside your process, your tools and your standups. Direction stays with you; we handle the employment relationship.
